About

I work on embodied robotic manipulation, with a focus on underactuated grippers, lightweight mechanisms, visuotactile sensing, and physically verifiable prototypes. My recent work includes Gamma grippers for thin-object scooping and adaptive grasping, TPL / LTO grippers for constrained-space industrial manipulation, and a GelSight-based dexterous-hand validation platform.

Research and Projects

Gamma Series GrippersScott-Russell and parallelogram mechanisms for thin-object pinching, scooping, and enveloping. Reported 94% asymmetric aluminum-foil scooping success and 98% daily-object grasping success.
TPL / LTO Constrained-Space GrippersTendon-driven grippers for combined pinching and lifting, designed for grooved motors, inserted bolts, and post-grasp extraction in constrained environments.
GelSight Dexterous-Hand PlatformSix-axis-arm-to-single-finger six-DOF validation platform with GelSight-based visuotactile reconstruction and force-control development.
Embodied Dexterous-Hand ProjectProvincial undergraduate innovation project on multimodal dexterous-hand design and autonomous operation.
